Sunlight crosses the atmosphere twice before reaching the satellite. In the O₂ A-band the absorber amount is known, so the band measures the light path (surface pressure, clouds, aerosols); in the two CO₂ bands the same path reveals how much CO₂ it crossed. Move XCO₂ and watch the CO₂ lines deepen while the O₂ band stays put. The dashed line is the reflected sunlight without any absorption.
