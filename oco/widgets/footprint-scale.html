Each OCO-2 sounding is a parallelogram about 1 km across and 2 km along the orbit; eight sit side by side across a swath only ~9 km wide, three frames every second. A single 99-minute orbit traces one thin line around the Earth, so the map is built from thousands of such lines over sixteen days. OCO-3 on the Space Station instead sweeps its pointing mirror to lay adjacent swaths over a city, a "snapshot area map".
